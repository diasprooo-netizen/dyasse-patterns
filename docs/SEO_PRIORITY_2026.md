Guidelines:
- One primary query per page; use natural variants as subheadings, no keyword stuffing.
- Distinct original copy, specs, stitch terms, skill level, measurements, photos of actual physical outcomes, FAQ.
- Product/Offer structured data only for genuinely published buyable products with legitimate pricing.
- Category pillar pages with internal links; crawlable links, sitemap, canonical, image alt text and stable slugs.
- Reuse owned photos only after content QC; avoid fake review markup and speculative ratings.
- Do not automatically publish 177 Etsy listings or market a pattern as human-made when it is AI-assisted.
- Link product pages from Pinterest pins and tutorials; SEO may take weeks/months.
