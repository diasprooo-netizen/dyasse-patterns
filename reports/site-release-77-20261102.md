Date: 2026-11-02.

## Decision and limits

- The seller explicitly requested that all 174 items with PDF files in Supabase be live on dyasse.com and sold with PayPal when Gumroad is unavailable or unpublished.
- This report identifies **77 of 80** remaining drafts whose title and description clearly describe a digital pattern; they are seller-approved for release. This is a release authorization and file-integrity preflight, **not an independent line-by-line crochet or knitting technical audit**.
- The PDFs were previously uploaded to the private Supabase bucket `dyasse-paid-patterns` during the Work intake. Work reviewer notes indicate structural reopening, first-page rendering and byte/hash recording, but the intake ledger remains `pending_final_review` and is preserved without erasure.
- The expected private PDF path, MIME type and byte count were matched against the stored object and intake ledger for each candidate. Positive USD catalog prices exactly match their PayPal cents. Unique product SKU, slug and cover image were checked.
- These 77 products can be bought using the site PayPal checkout and Supabase paid-file delivery. Gumroad draft links, where present, are not to be exposed as a verified active checkout. No Gumroad status flags are changed.
- **3 listings excluded** because their public title/description describe finished physical or custom-made goods and make shipping/personalization claims: `DYS-0097` dog and owner set, `DYS-0118` finished beach bag and `DYS-0140` custom laptop sleeve. They require confirmation that their stored PDF really contains instructions, followed by a rewritten digital-only listing before safe publication.
- The 94 previously published site products, 3 missing-PDF entries (including DYS-0107) and the Gumroad '(copy)' product are not edited by this release.
- We have not run a completed live PayPal purchase or independently proven post-capture email delivery; buyer-flow verification is still required.
